## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the @Watch App.

### Personal Information

The @Watch App does not collect, request, use, share, or otherwise have access to any personal information. It uses your device's clock and time zone information to display the Internet Time.

The app does not connect to, or transmit personal information over, the Internet - Internet Time doesn't actually require the Internet!

If enabled on your phone, App Store Analytics may provide us with error reports in the event of a crash. Error reports may include information about your device model.

### Changes
We may update this privacy policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to our practices or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons.
